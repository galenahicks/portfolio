National Theatre in America Membership Flyers

Below are various projects I created for National Theatre in America. Included are generic sign-up sheets to be handed out at NTiA events, as well as an invitation and sign-up sheet for NTiA's Inter Alia Gala. For the Inter Alia project, I was tasked to come up with a small identity for the Gala including color scheme, typeface choices and the Inter Alia wordmark that can be seen on both assets.
